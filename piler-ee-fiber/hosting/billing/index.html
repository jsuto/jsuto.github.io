    Hosting: usage reports for billing#

    This documentation applies to Piler enterprise edition 2.2.5+

    Revision #1

    Publication date: 2026-OCT-25

    Piler's license doesn't depend on your tenants' size, but your prices may: by the stored size, the number of mailboxes or the emails archived. Three ways to get the numbers per tenant.

    The quota dashboard#

    On the master, as the admin: Admin > Tenant > Quotas lists every tenant with its storage, emails and mailboxes, and Export CSV downloads them. The same page sets per-tenant quotas with warning emails. On a multi-node deployment the master collects the numbers from the workers.

    The REST API#

    For a script that feeds your billing system, the same data is at GET /api/v1/admin/quota/all, with a token of the master's admin:

    TOKEN=$(curl -s -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
   -d '{"username":"admin@local","password":"<password>"}' \
   https://archive.example.com/auth/login | jq -r .access_token)

curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" https://archive.example.com/api/v1/admin/quota/all \
   | jq -r '.[] | [.tenant_id, .storage_used_gb, .email_count, .user_count] | @csv'
    Field Meaning
    storage_used_gb Stored (compressed, encrypted) size of everything the tenant has archived, including email purged since
    email_count Emails received, all time
    user_count Mailboxes of the tenant's domains that received archived email

    GET /api/v1/admin/quota/monthly/<tenant id>?months=12 gives the emails received and the bytes stored per month, for usage-based prices. See the REST API for the details.

    Two things to know:

    • For tenants with a retention period, storage_used_gb grows past what is stored now, as purged email is still counted. For the current size, use the metrics below.
    • On a multi-node deployment, user_count adds up the workers' counts: a mailbox that received email on several workers is counted on each. With the email spread among the workers, count the mailboxes on one worker, or from the customer's directory.

    Metrics#

    From 2.2.6, piler-ui's /metrics has the current numbers per tenant, for your monitoring system to keep a history of (see monitoring):

    Metric Meaning
    piler_tenant_stored_bytes Stored size of what the tenant has archived now, purged email excluded
    piler_tenant_size_bytes Original size of the same
    piler_tenant_messages_received Emails received, all time

    On a multi-node deployment they come from the workers: sum them by tenant, e.g. sum by (tenant) (piler_tenant_stored_bytes). A monthly maximum or average of the stored size is a fair base for a storage price.
